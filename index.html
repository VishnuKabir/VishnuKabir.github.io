Vishnu Kabir Chhabra

PhD Candidate · AI Safety & Mechanistic Interpretability

I'm a PhD candidate at The Ohio State University, advised by Prof. Xueru Zhang and Prof. Mohammad Mahdi Khalili, working on AI safety through mechanistic interpretability. I study how safety mechanisms in language models, such as refusal behavior and steering directions, change under compression, fine-tuning and corruption, and I build lightweight methods that make models safer.

10 papers, 6 first-author, at NAACL, MobiCom, CSCW and ICML workshops. Currently open to research scientist roles in AI safety and interpretability.

04 selected projects

Cross-Band Channel Prediction

2022

Python · PyTorch · Diffusion models

End-to-end framework built on a conditional diffusion model that beats the prior state of the art by 2 dB SNR for downlink channel prediction; collaborated on the physics-based HORCRUX approach (MobiCom 2024).

Face Recognition for Dental-Assisting Robots

2023

Python · PyTorch

Generated synthetic benchmark data with Stable Diffusion and evaluated AdaFace and MogFace, reaching 90% accuracy for dual-factor authentication.

Food Calorie Estimation with WiFi

2023

MATLAB · Python · Spectral analysis

Estimated calories and nutrients of liquid and solid foods from WiFi signals with up to 10% error, robust to interference and food containers.

Deep RL for Automated Trading

2022

Undergraduate thesis

Benchmarked DQN, DDPG, PPO, SAC, A2C, TD3 and multi-agent DDPG for cryptocurrency trading, modeled as a POMDP. Awarded a Distinguished Poster.
